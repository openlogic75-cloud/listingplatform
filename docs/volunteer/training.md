# Volunteer training — how site visits work

> Source of truth for the "How site visits work" card in the app's volunteer
> home. `local-market.md` promises volunteers "training and TA/DA": TA/DA is
> covered by the verification fee (Q6 — admin-set, paid directly to the
> visiting volunteer, snapshotted on the badge in M5.4). This guide is the
> training part. Keep the app card's wording in sync with this file.

## 1. Schedule the visit with the vendor

- Pick up a report from the volunteer home screen only when you can actually
  travel there. The platform's service areas are the localities the admin has
  marked active — do not promise visits outside them.
- Agree a day and time with the vendor by phone. Visits are in person; there
  is no remote verification.

## 2. Check the site

Confirm and note each of these — they are the checklist the admin reads:

1. **Seller identity** — the person present matches the vendor name on the
   listing.
2. **Address** — the site is where the vendor said it is. Record the geo
   point from your phone in the report.
3. **Goods** — the listed products exist and match the description
   (quantity, condition; for rentals/homestays, the room/space and its
   availability).

## 3. Take evidence photos

- Photograph the shop/site front, the goods, and anything that proves the
  checklist answers (signboards, stock, rooms).
- Upload through the report screen (max 8). Photos are stored as your report
  evidence and shown to the admin — that is their only use.
- Photograph goods and places, not people, unless the person agrees and it
  is needed for the check.

## 4. File the report from the volunteer home screen

- Write plain, specific notes: what you saw, in your own words. No guesses.
- Submit when the report is complete. You can keep drafts; only submitted
  reports reach the admin.
- If something was wrong (wrong address, goods missing), say so clearly —
  a rejected report with honest notes is a good report.

## 5. Approval, badge, and your visit fee

- The admin approves or rejects in the review queue. Approval issues the
  verified badge naming **you** ("verified by: your name"), which survives
  even if your account data is later deleted (DPDP rule, M5.3).
- The visit fee — set by the admin, shown before the visit is booked — is
  paid to **you directly by the vendor at the visit**. The platform never
  touches it (Q1-style offline settlement; money boundary, AGENTS.md §7).

## Ground rules

- **Honest notes only.** Never approve-wash a visit; the badge carries your
  name.
- **Minimum data.** Collect nothing beyond the report fields. Guest and
  vendor contact details stay encrypted and you never see more than your
  own queue needs.
- **Consent first.** Say who you are and why you are photographing before
  you start.
- **Safety.** Visit during the agreed daytime slot; leave if anything feels
  off and note it in the report instead.
